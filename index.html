Our shop

TK SON HALWA is a family-run counter. We make Multani-style soan halwa in a copper degh each morning and sell it by the kilo.

We do not keep stock in cold storage. When the day's batch is finished, we take orders for the next morning. Message us and we will tell you what is ready.
