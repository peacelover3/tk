# Surface brief: home (index.html) + detail (halwa.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: local Pakistani customers arriving from WhatsApp
forwards or Instagram links, mostly mobile, often on mobile data. Job:
show the halwa range with honest per-kilo PKR prices; action: open wa.me
to +92 336 5505467 with the prefilled order message. Proof: real halwa
photography (stock now, owner replaces), visible prices, plain-spoken
copy, WhatsApp as the native ordering channel. Constraints: static files,
no build, all editable copy in assets/data.js, no invented credentials,
no em-dashes in visible text, English copy only.

Direction: "From the Degh" (own grounded candidate, ROLL lead, seed db24b3e0).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page sells one kilo of halwa the way the shop makes it: you
follow one batch from the copper degh to the price plate. It refuses the
category-default arrangement, a grid of food cards under a banner hero.

OWN-WORLD: warm wheat-paper ground (page #F5EEDF range, cards a shade
lighter), deep walnut ink for text and primary controls, one saffron-gold
accent for prices and stamps, hairline rules like a shop ledger. Type:
warm grotesque display (Cabinet Grotesk) over a plain humanist body
(Satoshi), price numerals oversized and tabular. Photos sit in soft
rounded frames; chips and buttons are pill, cards 16px, inputs soft.

STORY: the visitor understands that Multani-style soan halwa is made here
daily in one copper degh, sold by the kilo, packed fresh, and orderable
through one WhatsApp tap; they believe the prices because every price is
printed plainly, per kilo, no games.

FIRST VIEWPORT: top bar 68px with brand caps left, quiet anchors and an
Order pill right. Hero splits 7/5: left column carries a two-line
headline, one 20-word subline, and two CTAs (See the menu, WhatsApp);
right column shows a large halwa disc photo in a rimmed round frame with
an overlapping Rs-per-kg weight stamp, warm steam motion. CTA visible
without scroll.

FORM: chosen "From the Degh", first of seven grounded structures, seed
key db24b3e0. Donations kept from declined catalog worlds: ledger
hairlines (plate section), weight-stamped numerals (nixie), pinned price
tags (magazine call-outs).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with
the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster
carrying its provenance.

Unresolved decisions: exact PKR prices are owner drafts; delivery area
wording is a place-holder the owner edits in data.js; photos are stock
stand-ins tagged for replacement.
